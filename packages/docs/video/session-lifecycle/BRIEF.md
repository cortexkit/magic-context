# Session lifecycle

The supplied brief authorizes an autonomous build and a real local MP4 render. The storyboard is a copy-review deliverable, not a pending approval gate.

## Direction

Keep one context-window bar on screen throughout. Use dark ground, cyan live work, violet history, amber memory, and neutral system instructions. Use large declarative captions; make the whole story work silently. No stock media, avatars, marketing language, public upload, or deployment.

## Required sequence

Fresh session → queued reduction and small dropped placeholders → background historian and prompt-resident compartments → 65% execute trigger and batched passes → cache stability → search and raw recall → the same session after hours of work.

## Decisions

84 seconds, 1920×1080, 30 fps. No audio: captions carry the story, without a voice service or credentials. All sizes are illustrative, not telemetry. The 45% worked example follows the docs; the later ~60% outcome is explicitly illustrative as requested. History's default 15% budget is a share of context at the execute threshold, not 15% of the entire window. Earlier reductions land on explicitly labeled cache-rebuilding passes, never instantly on a tool call. Use a single composition instead of separate frame workers so the bar never cuts away.
