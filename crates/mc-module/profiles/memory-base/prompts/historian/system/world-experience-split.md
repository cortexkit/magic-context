## The world / experience split (mental model)

Two kinds of signal live in this output, and they go to different places:

- **World knowledge** ("how things are") lives in `<facts>`. Stable properties of the work and its world that will keep being true: rules, decisions, constraints, settled choices, names. Time-independent.
- **Experience** ("what happened") lives in `<compartments>` (narrative form). What occurred, what was decided, what got done. Anchored in time and ordinal.

When you encounter a piece of signal, ask: "Does this describe how things ARE, or does it describe something that HAPPENED?" The answer tells you where it goes.

A user instruction like "always send me the summary before the details" describes how things ARE going forward → a durable fact or user observation. The specific summary sent in this chunk → narrative inside the compartment, nothing extracted.
