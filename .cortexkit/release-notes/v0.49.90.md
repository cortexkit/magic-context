# v0.49.90

This patch fixes three ways an outdated checkpoint record could hide messages or refuse a session on OpenCode 2. OpenCode 1 and Pi are not affected and do not change.

## Upgrading

Update the plugin with `npx @cortexkit/magic-context@latest doctor`, then restart your host. No database migration is needed.

## Fixed

- **OpenCode 2: messages could be missing from requests after the history got shorter.** An OpenCode 2 session keeps a record of where the stored conversation continues after the history block. When the summaries later covered less than that record claimed, the messages in between were sent neither as messages nor as summaries. The store was intact, and nothing told you. We reproduced it three ways: a `/ctx-recomp` that stopped partway after a manual `/compact` (28 messages), a `/ctx-recomp` that stopped partway in a session converted from OpenCode 1 (37 messages), and a completed `/ctx-recomp` followed by a revert in such a session (17 messages). Magic Context now ignores a record that points past the history it rendered, serves the stored messages from the real position, and saves the corrected record on that request. Present since 0.49.47. 0.49.89 already stopped a partial `/ctx-recomp` from shortening the history; this release repairs sessions that are already in that state and covers the other ways into it.
- **OpenCode 2: a session could refuse every turn with `MC-S06` after a revert past its summaries.** The sequence was an early `/compact`, later summaries, a second `/compact`, and a revert that removed the messages those summaries ended on. Now a revert retires the summaries of the messages it removed, and a session with no summaries and no usable record starts again from its first stored message. A session that is already refused this way recovers on its next turn without a command.

## What to expect after the update

- **One prompt rebuild per affected session.** The first request after the correction rebuilds the cached prompt once. In a session that was refused on every turn and still has summaries, the second request changes once more, because one host instruction line is now covered by a summary. Magic Context logs that change once.
- **Restored messages can appear as `[dropped §N§]`.** Messages that were marked as no longer needed before the history got shorter stay reduced. They come back as placeholders, or stay removed if a tool call and its result were removed together before, until a summary covers them again. `ctx_expand` with the tag or the message still returns the original. Search and range `ctx_expand` treat these messages as visible; that is a known limit and not new in this release.
- **A large session may refuse one or two turns while it catches up.** In our test, a session with 75,000 tokens of unsummarized messages in a 60,000-token window was refused for two turns while summaries were written, and served at 55,700 tokens on the third. No command or restart was needed.
- **One limit.** The proof that a revert removed a message is kept in memory. If the host restarts right after the revert, while a summary run is still in progress, the old `MC-S06` refusal can remain. Run `/ctx-recomp` to the end to clear it.
- **Healthy sessions do not change.** On OpenCode 2.0.22, 2.1.0, 2.3.2 and 2.4.2, requests of sessions without this damage are byte-identical with and without this fix.
